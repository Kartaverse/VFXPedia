This site is operated by [eyeon Software](http://www.eyeonline.com/) as a free resource to artists who make use of our software, and the compositing community in general. The best donation you can possibly make to the success of this site is to get involved. Create an account, then contribute new information, or expand on the information already present on the site.


**However**, if you simply feel you must contribute to the development of the VFXPedia in a more concrete fasion, the hardworking staff of eyeon Software Inc. has indicated its willingness to accept donations in the form of beer. As a group we enjoy all sorts of beer, though [Coopers Sparkling Ale](http://www.coopers.com.au/) from Australia is a clear favorite amongst the development staff.
